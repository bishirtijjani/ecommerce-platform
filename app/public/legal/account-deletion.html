Delete Your Account

Last updated: 12 October 2026

You can delete your MySooq account at any time. This page explains how to do it and what happens to your information. It applies to the MySooq app and the website at mysooq.ng.

Delete from inside the app

  1. Open the MySooq app and sign in.
  2. Go to your Profile.
  3. Scroll down to the Danger zone.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm.

If you have active orders, finish or cancel them first, then delete your account. If you cannot sign in, use the email option below.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access your account, email support@mysooq.ng from the email address linked to your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We may ask you to verify your identity before we act, to protect your account from unauthorised deletion.

What happens when you delete your account

  • Your account is deactivated and you are signed out of all devices.
  • Your personal details are removed from your account, including your name, email address, phone number, profile photo and saved addresses.
  • Your account can no longer be used to sign in.

What we keep, and for how long

We retain order and transaction records, and records needed to meet legal, tax, accounting and dispute-resolution obligations, for as long as the law requires (typically up to six years). These records are kept in a form that no longer identifies you as the account holder where possible. Messages and support records may be retained where needed to resolve disputes or prevent fraud.

How long it takes

In-app deletion takes effect immediately. Email requests are usually processed within 30 days of verifying your identity.

Questions

Contact support@mysooq.ng. See also our Privacy Policy.
