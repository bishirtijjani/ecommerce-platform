Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Marketplace ("Marketplace", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects your personal information when you use the Marketplace mobile app and the website at marketplace.ng (together, the "Service"). It is written to meet the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR).

1. Who we are

The data controller responsible for your personal information is Marketplace, trading in Nigeria. You can reach our privacy team at support@marketplace.ng.

Marketplace is a marketplace. It connects buyers with independent vendors. Vendors are separate businesses and are responsible for the information they handle about you in the course of selling and fulfilling their goods and services.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: first name, last name, email address, phone number and password (stored only in hashed form).
  • Profile information: profile photo, and optionally your date of birth.
  • Addresses: saved delivery and pickup addresses, including state and local government area.
  • Content you create: messages you exchange with vendors, support tickets and disputes you open, requests you post, reviews and ratings, and any photos you upload (for example a profile photo or an attachment to a support ticket).
  • Orders: details of orders placed with or logged for you by vendors.

Information collected automatically

  • Location: if you grant permission, your device's approximate or precise location, used to show nearby vendors and delivery options. You can turn location off in your device settings at any time.
  • Device and technical data: device name and model, operating system and version, app version, and a device identifier that we attach to requests so we can secure your account and detect abuse.
  • Push token: a notification token from your device so we can send you order and account notifications.
  • Diagnostics: crash and error reports that help us fix problems. These are configured to exclude passwords, PINs, tokens and other secrets.

We do not use advertising identifiers, we do not run third-party advertising or behavioural-tracking SDKs, and we do not sell your personal information.

3. How we use your information

  • Create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • Operate the marketplace: show vendors and products, deliver orders, enable chat with vendors, and provide support.
  • Show nearby results and delivery options when you allow location access.
  • Send transactional and service messages (for example OTP codes, order updates and support replies) by push, email or SMS.
  • Keep the Service safe: detect and prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents.
  • Comply with legal, tax and accounting obligations, and resolve disputes.
  • Improve and troubleshoot the Service.

4. Legal bases

We process your information to perform our contract with you (providing the Service), to comply with legal obligations, for our legitimate interests (securing and improving the Service, preventing fraud), and on the basis of your consent where required (for example location access and push notifications, which you can withdraw at any time).

5. Sharing your information

We share personal information only as needed:

  • With vendors you choose to contact or order from, so they can respond and fulfil your order.
  • With service providers that process data on our behalf, including hosting and content delivery (Cloudflare), image storage (Cloudinary), push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging and Expo), crash diagnostics (Sentry), mapping (Google Maps), and email/SMS delivery providers. These providers act on our instructions.
  • With payment providers. If a payment feature is used, it is handled by licensed payment providers. The app does not collect or store your card details.
  • For legal reasons, where required by law or to protect rights, safety and security.

6. International transfers

Some of our service providers may process information outside Nigeria. Where this happens, we take steps to ensure your information receives an adequate level of protection, consistent with the Nigeria Data Protection Act.

7. How long we keep information

We keep your account information while your account is active. If you delete your account, we anonymise your personal details and deactivate the account. We retain order and transaction records where the law requires it (for example, tax and accounting records, typically for up to six years). See our Account Deletion page for details.

8. How we protect information

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), hashed passwords, access controls, and regular review of our systems. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and to notify you and the relevant authorities of a breach where required.

9. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act, you have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • request a copy of your information in a portable form; and
  • complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

To exercise any of these rights, email support@marketplace.ng. You can also delete your account directly in the app (see below).

10. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from within the app: open your Profile, scroll to the Danger zone, and choose Delete account. You can also request deletion by emailing support@marketplace.ng. Full details are on our Account Deletion page.

11. Children

The Service is for adults. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Cookies and local storage

The website and app use local storage on your device to keep you signed in and to remember your preferences (such as language and theme). We do not use advertising cookies.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version here and change the "Last updated" date. Where changes are significant, we will notify you in the app.

14. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your personal information? Email support@marketplace.ng.
