### Full description

> MySooq is the local marketplace that connects you with real vendors near
> you - clothes, phones, vehicles, home items, beauty, groceries, second-hand
> deals and more. Browse, compare, and talk to sellers directly. One app.

**Discover what's around you**
- Browse by category or search across thousands of listings
- See shops and vendors near you, with verified-seller badges
- New "China Market", "Arab World" and "Wholesale" storefronts for imported
  and bulk goods
- A dedicated Second Hand section with location filters by state and LGA

**Shop the way you want**
- Compare prices, condition (new / used / 2nd-hand) and ratings at a glance
- Save items to your wishlist and come back to them later
- Message any seller in-app - ask about price, stock or availability
- Call the vendor or request a callback without leaving the app

**Can't find it? Ask for it.**
- Post a request describing exactly what you need, with photos
- Choose how far sellers should be able to see it - nearby or anywhere
- Set a budget and urgency, then receive offers from sellers
- Accept the best offer and message the seller straight away

**Turn your shop into a business**
- Open your own shop and list your products on MySooq
- Manage your catalogue, orders and storefront from the seller dashboard
- Reach buyers looking for exactly what you sell - including bulk/wholesale

**Built for Nigeria**
- Pay-as-you-go - free to use
- Available in English and Hausa
- Works on slow networks; skeleton loaders, not spinners
- In-app support and a clear dispute process if something goes wrong

MySooq is a marketplace. It connects buyers with independent vendors; vendors
are separate businesses, not agents of MySooq.

**Permissions we ask for, and why**
- Location: to show listings, shops and vendors near you
- Camera + photo library: to add photos to listings, chats and support
  requests, and to set your profile photo
- Notifications: order updates, seller replies and messages

**Need help?** Tap *Account → Help & Support* in the app, or email
support@mysooq.ng.
